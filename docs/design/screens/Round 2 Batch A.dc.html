Every frame is 1440 wide (one at 768 for the header) and built only from tokens.css and the round 1 components. Frames are live: the "Demo only" switcher, review mode, Approve, Correct a value, Waive, Reject, resend and W-9 buttons all work inside each frame.

Checked against §3, §5, §7

  • Sample data: large badge beside the app name on every frame, plus a small badge beside every figure (worklist headline, record header, values card).
  • Demo only: the round 1 switcher sits in the header of every frame.
  • Status words: every pill, line result (Pass, Fail, Waived) and hold flag carries its word and a shape. Red only on Expired, fail and the deficiency list.
  • Numbers: only "Compliant X of Y expected" (§7, per project). Reading confidence and the 85% line come from §6.4. Limits and dates are values read off a certificate, not metrics.
  • Vocabulary: status, waiting-for and role words exactly as §5. No acronyms but W-9 and PDF. No model or vendor names.
  • Sources: plain tab names (Compliance records, Certificates, Projects, Activity log) with a timestamp beside each figure.

Batch A review: what changed

  • Summit Mechanical is now Expiring soon, waiting for Needs review: the status comes from the current certificate. Changed in the worklist, the record header and the pill.
  • Headline is now "Compliant 32 of 35 expected". It is computed from the rows, so it updates on its own.
  • Requirement lines now match evaluateCoi: the six limits, then "Printed" or "Not printed" for Additional insured endorsement, Project address and Certificate holder, then Insured name as "Matches" or "Does not match" with both names in small text underneath, then Cancellation notice. The Expiry date line is gone. The policy period and expiry date now sit in the record header.
  • Email and deficiency text is the code's fixed text, word for word. Success messages are the code's, including "W-9 marked on file." and "W-9 marked missing."
  • Status after Reject or resend follows the current certificate when there is one. See 4n for a rejected renewal that stays Compliant. Otherwise the status is Awaiting.
  • W-9: marking it on file drops only that reason. See 4o: Birchwood stays on hold for "Policy expired". Marking it missing adds no hold until the next pay-cycle check.
  • Review mode help now uses the code's behaviour. Fieldstone now reads "The system sends reminders".
  • All nine new components are in Components.dc.html, section 03.

Still open

  2. Birchwood now has two hold reasons (Policy expired; No W-9 on file), to show the partial release. The Batch B hold list will use the same data.
