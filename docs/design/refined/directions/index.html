Three directions for the compliance record

The same Round 2 screen, data, wording and rules in three visual languages. Each one is live: switch the role, correct a value, approve, waive or reject. Pick one and it becomes the shared stylesheet for every screen.

A

Built for the accountant who works this queue all day. Type is small and tight, rows are short, and a dark side rail keeps navigation out of the work area. Every action shows its key on the button: A approves, E opens a correction, Ctrl+Enter saves, and J and K walk the value lines while the PDF follows. Motion is quick and mechanical, so the screen confirms what changed without slowing anyone down. A CFO opening it cold may find it busy.

  • IBM Plex Sans + Mono
  • 13 px base
  • 90 / 160 / 240 ms
  • Keys on buttons
B

Built to be trusted with money decisions. Amounts sit right-aligned in tabular figures, borders are few, and the next step is the only raised object on the page, so the eye goes there first. Three panes put the certificate, the values and the decision side by side, and the decision stays in view while you scroll. Motion is slower and softer and lands without bounce. It looks the most like a product a firm would pay for. It shows fewer rows than A, and its keyboard support stays out of sight until someone presses ?.

  • Geist
  • 14 px base
  • 120 / 220 / 360 ms
  • One raised panel
C

Built around the evidence. The certificate takes half the screen on a dark viewing surface. Point at any value and the same spot on the page lights up like a highlighter mark, with a red edge when that line fails. That shows the product's promise in one gesture: code checks the reading against the paper, and a person signs off. The demo controls float in their own dashed panel, well away from the product. The dark pane is the one bold move, and it may feel heavy on a small laptop.

  • Instrument Sans + DM Mono
  • 14 px base
  • 120 / 200 / 320 ms
  • Highlighter link

Keyboard (all three)

  • A approve · E correct a value
  • Ctrl ↵ save or waive · Esc cancel
  • J K move through lines · ? all keys

The same in every direction

  • Round 2 data, states, wording and check rules. No new numbers.
  • Status always in words with a shape. Red only for fail, Expired and At risk.
  • Figures never animate. Reduced motion turns movement into a short fade.
